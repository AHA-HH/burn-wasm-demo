1D Burgers Fourier Neural Operator

Inference runs entirely in your browser (WebAssembly). Pick an initial condition a(x), and the FNO predicts u(x, T) — at any resolution, without retraining.

Ground truth u_true(x) is overlaid, with a real relative L2 error at training resolution.

a(x) = amplitude · sin(2π · wavenumber · x) — smooth by construction, well inside the training distribution.

Draw a(x) freehand. On release it's projected onto the first 16 Fourier modes before reaching the model — a hand-drawn curve is far outside the smooth training distribution otherwise.
